M5Paper S3 Home Assistant panel

A clickable mock-up of the e-ink control panel: room lights with brightness and scenes, and Sonos speakers with playlists, volume and zone grouping. Nothing here talks to a real home.

Try it

  • Tap a room to switch all its lights on or off.
  • Hold a room for 0.6 s to open its lights, then tap a light or tap along its bar to set the brightness (10 % steps).
  • Kitchen and Living Room also have scenes.
  • On Sonos, tick rooms on the left, then use a playlist, the volume buttons or "Saml alle zoner" (group all zones). The ⏭ button skips to the next track (not shown for radio).

Touch areas are outlined in orange. "Powered off" is what the e-ink keeps showing after the panel switches itself off. "No connection" appears when Home Assistant can't be reached for 30 s (the time is a setting in HA).

The real panel

  • ESPHome firmware with LVGL on the 4.7" 540×960 e-ink, pure black and white for fast refreshes (~0.25 s).
  • Up to 12 areas, each with 6 lights, 4 scenes and temperature / humidity / CO₂, all set from Home Assistant.
  • Sleeps with Wi-Fi off; a tap or picking it up wakes it, and taps made while it reconnects are queued.
